Crest Ocean System foam adaptation
Upstream: https://github.com/wave-harmonic/crest
Revision: db0658ff0b2e93e4a9e28cc2867509658b0ecc00

CrestFoamSimulation.java adapts UpdateFoam.compute (flow advection, decay,
compression-driven deposition) and OceanFoam.hlsl (WhiteFoamTexture black-point
fade). CrestFoamAppearance.java shares the foam appearance and texture sampling
between coastal waves and wakes. AET supplies the local crest displacement and carries the foam material
coordinates with the density. Unity rendering, ocean LODs, and shader-pack wave
sampling are not included.

textures/particle/crest_foam.png is an unmodified copy of
crest/Assets/Crest/Crest/Textures/Foam2.png.
